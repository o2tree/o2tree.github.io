Day 1Sunday 4 Oct

Arrive Taipei, Go to Chiayi

  1. 11:45

    Arrive at Taoyuan (TPE)

    Clear immigration, buy an EasyCard, SIM/eSIM and withdraw some TWD.

  2. 13:15

    Airport MRT to Taoyuan HSR Station

    About 20 min; this skips Taipei entirely. Buy a HSR ticket to Chiayi (book ahead if possible).

  3. 14:30

    Taiwan High Speed Rail to Chiayi

    About 1h15 from Taoyuan. Take the bus/taxi from Chiayi HSR Station to your hotel in the city.

  4. 16:30

    Beimen Station & Forest Railway Garden

    Historic Alishan Forest Railway terminus and old railway buildings; check-in first if you like.

  5. 18:30

    Wenhua Road Night Market

    Try Chiayi's signature turkey rice (Chiayi chicken rice), fish-head soup and local snacks. Early night.

Day 2Monday 5 Oct

Chiayi to Alishan (overnight)

  1. 08:00

    Bus: Chiayi to Alishan

    About 2.5-3 hours from Chiayi Station or HSR Station; buy tickets early as seats can sell out. Check the current timetable.

  2. 11:30

    Arrive Alishan, drop bags

    Stay inside or near the scenic area to make tomorrow's sunrise easy.

  3. 12:30

    Lunch in Alishan

    Try local mountain tea and simple dishes.

  4. 14:00

    Giant Trees Trail & Sacred Tree

    Walk among ancient cypress trees, temples and forest boardwalks.

  5. 16:00

    Sisters Ponds & Shouzhen Temple

    Easy, scenic short walks.

  6. 17:30

    Sunset & dinner

    Early night; you'll wake before dawn.

Day 3Tuesday 6 Oct

Alishan Sunrise & Return to Taipei

  1. 04:00

    Wake up for sunrise

    Take the Alishan sunrise train to Zhushan (Chushan) or a shuttle; book ahead.

  2. 05:30

    Sunrise & sea of clouds

    Bring a jacket; it can be around 10-15 C at dawn.

  3. 08:00

    Breakfast & last forest walk

    Optional: Alishan Forest Railway station area and tea shops.

  4. 10:30

    Bus back to Chiayi HSR Station

    About 2.5-3 hours.

  5. 14:30

    HSR from Chiayi to Taipei

    About 1h40. Check in at your Taipei hotel.

  6. 17:30

    Longshan Temple & Ximending

    Easy first look at Taipei after the mountains.

  7. 19:30

    Ningxia or Huaxi Night Market

    Oyster omelette, fried chicken, taro balls.

Day 4Wednesday 7 Oct

Taipei City Highlights

  1. 09:00

    Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall

    Grand plaza and changing-of-the-guard ceremony on the hour.

  2. 11:00

    Yongkang Street

    Cafes and small eateries; try beef noodle soup here.

  3. 13:00

    Din Tai Fung

    Famous soup dumplings; the Xinyi branch is a good option.

  4. 14:30

    Taipei 101

    Observatory views; book tickets online to skip queues.

  5. 17:00

    Elephant Mountain

    20-30 min climb for the classic skyline view at sunset.

  6. 19:30

    Raohe Street Night Market

    Try the pepper buns near the temple entrance.

Day 5Thursday 8 Oct

Taipei-Jiufen-Shifen Tour (0.5 day) & Departure

  1. 08:30

    Tour pick-up in Taipei

    Half-day guided tour (about 5-6 hours) or DIY by train/bus; meet at Taipei Main Station or Ximending. Check out first and store luggage at the hotel.

  2. 10:00

    Jiufen Old Street

    About 1.5-2 hours: hillside teahouses, lanterns, Shengping Theatre and peanut ice cream rolls.

  3. 12:15

    Shifen Old Street (Shihfen)

    About 1 hour: release a sky lantern along the railway tracks; optional Shifen Waterfall if time allows.

  4. 14:00

    Return to Taipei

    Tour ends in central Taipei by about 14:30-15:00.

  5. 15:30

    Collect bags & souvenir shopping

    Buy pineapple cakes, tea and nougat, e.g. around Dihua Street or Taipei Main Station.

  6. 18:00

    Early dinner

    One last favourite, such as beef noodles or hot pot.

  7. 20:00

    Airport MRT to TPE

    Aim to arrive by 20:45 for check-in and duty free.

  8. 23:50

    Depart on TR893

    Arrive Singapore 04:15 on 9 Oct.

Travel tips

  • Route: Landing at Taoyuan and taking the HSR straight from Taoyuan station saves a trip into Taipei. Chiayi HSR Station is outside the city, so allow time for the bus or taxi.
  • Where to stay: Chiayi city (Day 1), Alishan (Day 2), then Taipei Main Station area for Days 3-5 (HSR hub and airport access). Book Alishan early; options are limited.
  • Alishan bookings: Reserve HSR, buses, sunrise train and hotel ahead of time. Check current Alishan Forest Railway status before you go, as some sections have been affected by typhoons.
  • Alishan weather: Much cooler than Taipei and often misty or rainy; pack a warm layer, rain jacket and comfortable walking shoes.
  • Luggage: You'll carry bags for 3 days; ask your hotels about storage and pack light for the mountain.
  • Getting around: Get an EasyCard for MRT, buses and convenience stores. The Airport MRT runs until about midnight.
  • Weather: Early October can bring typhoons; keep a backup plan if mountain transport is disrupted.
  • What changed: The itinerary now starts with Chiayi and Alishan; Yehliu, the National Palace Museum, Tamsui, Maokong and Beitou were dropped.
  • Money: Night markets and small shops are often cash-only; carry some TWD.
  • Departure: Leave the hotel around 19:30-20:00 for a comfortable buffer before the 23:50 flight.
